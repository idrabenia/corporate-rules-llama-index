Remote Work Policy

Working from home is allowed up to 3 days per week and must be agreed with your manager at least 24 hours in advance.
Remote work requires a stable VPN connection and approval from the IT department (ticket type "Network access").
Overseas travel of more than 14 days requires security clearance from the Information Security Officer.
Company laptops are encrypted and may not be shared with family members.
If you work fully from home for business travel reasons for more than 30 days in a year, you must obtain tax advice from the finance team.
