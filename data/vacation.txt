Vacation

Employees accrue 2.33 days of paid vacation per full month of employment, which equals 28 days per calendar year.
Vacation requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance.
At least one of the two summer months (July or August) should be kept free; no more than half of the team may be on vacation at the same time.
Unused vacation days may be carried over to the next year only for up to 10 days and expire after March 31.
