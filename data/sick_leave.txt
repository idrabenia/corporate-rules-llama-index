Sick Leave and Unplanned Days Off

Sick leave requires a doctor's certificate if you are absent for more than 3 consecutive days.
Unplanned days off must be reported to your manager before 8:30 AM on the same day.
Bereavement leave: up to 3 paid days for immediate family, 1 day for other relatives, and 1 day to attend a funeral of a colleague.
Maternity and paternity leave follow local law and are fully documented by HR; notify HR at least 30 days before the expected start date.
