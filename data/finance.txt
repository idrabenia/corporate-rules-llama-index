Finance and Expenses

Travel expenses up to $300 per day do not require pre-approval; higher amounts need a manager sign-off.
Expense reports are due within 14 days after the trip ends; late submissions are reimbursed in the next pay cycle.
Commuting costs are not reimbursed except for business trips. Company cars may only be used between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM on business days.
