Working Hours and Breaks

Regular working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, including a 1-hour lunch break.
Lunch is provided by the company in the cafeteria on the 2nd floor and is served between 12:00 PM and 1:00 PM.
Employees are allowed two 15-minute coffee breaks per day, which should not overlap with team stand-ups.
The office kitchen closes at 6:30 PM on Fridays for cleaning.
